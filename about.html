About Ostrivo

Ostrivo is an AI-powered business intelligence tool built for people who have data but no analyst. Upload a CSV or Excel file and, in under 30 seconds, you get a cleaned dataset, interactive dashboards, anomaly detection, and a plain-English executive summary - with analysis tailored to your industry.

It's a live, working product with real users, multi-user accounts, and ongoing development - not a prototype.
